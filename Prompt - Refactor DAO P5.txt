Refactor the code to use Spring dependency injection instead of manually retrieving beans from `SpringContextHolder` or `ApplicationContext`.

Requirements:
- Remove usages of `SpringContextHolder.getApplicationContext().getBean(...)`.
- Replace manual bean lookups with constructor injection (preferred) or `@Autowired` where constructor injection is not feasible.

- Eliminate the need for static bean access wherever possible.
- Preserve existing behavior without changing business logic.
- Do not introduce unnecessary utility classes or wrappers.
